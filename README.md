# API-Industry-Market-Analysis-Business-Case
# Strategy & Business Internship | Jakson Group

A strategic business analytics and market intelligence portfolio project inspired by work undertaken during a **Strategy & Business Internship at Jakson Group**, covering large-scale infrastructure projects and an API manufacturing business case.

The repository demonstrates the application of **SQL analytics, financial modeling, market research, competitive benchmarking, KPI development, value engineering, and strategic analysis** to support business decision-making, operational improvement, and capital-allocation decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project covers three interconnected areas:

1. **Infrastructure Project Analytics**
2. **API Manufacturing Market Intelligence**
3. **Strategic & Financial Business Analysis**

The combined infrastructure portfolio represents approximately **₹4,000 Cr** in project value, while the API business case evaluates the attractiveness of entering and scaling within the pharmaceutical API ecosystem.

The objective was to translate operational and market data into **actionable business insights for management and strategic decision-making**.

---

# 🏗️ 1. Infrastructure Project Analytics

## Business Context

The analysis focuses on two large-scale infrastructure projects, each valued at approximately **₹2,000 Cr**.

The objective was to improve project execution, monitor government tender performance, identify cost-optimization opportunities, and provide management with structured KPI reporting.

### Key Business Problems

* Improving project delivery timelines
* Monitoring government tender execution
* Identifying project delays and bottlenecks
* Tracking contractor performance
* Monitoring CAPEX deployment
* Identifying cost overruns
* Developing executive-level project KPIs
* Supporting value-engineering initiatives

---

## Key Contributions & Outcomes

### 📊 Project Performance

* Supported KPI design and performance reporting across **two infrastructure projects worth ₹2,000 Cr each**.
* Developed milestone-level tracking to monitor project execution and identify deviations.
* Improved visibility into government tender adherence, supporting **95% on-time execution**.
* Identified operational bottlenecks contributing to project delays.

### ⏱️ Delivery Optimization

* Applied milestone and execution analytics to identify recurring sources of delays.
* Supported initiatives resulting in an estimated **20% reduction in project delays**.

### 💰 Cost Optimization

* Applied value-engineering frameworks to project activities and cost components.
* Identified approximately **3–5% cost-optimization opportunities** across relevant project areas.
* Analyzed CAPEX utilization and expenditure patterns to improve capital deployment visibility.

# 💊 2. API Manufacturing Business Case

## Business Context

The second component evaluates the attractiveness of the **Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API)** manufacturing industry and potential opportunities across domestic manufacturing, exports, CRDMO, and supply-chain localization.

The analysis combines market intelligence, competitor benchmarking, financial analysis, and government-policy research.

---

## Key Business Questions

* How attractive is the API manufacturing market?
* Which API segments offer the strongest growth opportunities?
* How competitive is the Indian API ecosystem?
* What are the profitability differences between major players?
* What opportunities exist in **CRDMO**?
* How can Indian manufacturers benefit from localization?
* What role do **PLI incentives** play in investment decisions?
* Which export markets offer potential expansion opportunities?

---

## 🔎 Market Intelligence

* Conducted competitive benchmarking across **50+ API and pharmaceutical companies**.
* Compared:

  * Revenue growth
  * EBITDA margins
  * Market share
  * Business segments
  * Export exposure
  * Growth trajectories
* Evaluated industry trends and competitive positioning.
* Assessed opportunities across API manufacturing and CRDMO services.

---

## 🏭 Strategic Analysis

The business case evaluates:

### CRDMO Opportunity

Analysis of the potential for Indian pharmaceutical manufacturers to expand into **Contract Research, Development and Manufacturing Organization (CRDMO)** services.

### Supply-Chain Localization

Assessment of opportunities arising from global efforts to diversify pharmaceutical supply chains and increase domestic manufacturing capabilities.

### Government Incentives

Evaluation of policy support, including **Production Linked Incentive (PLI)** schemes, and their potential impact on manufacturing economics and investment attractiveness.

### Export Opportunity

Analysis of export concentration, market exposure, and potential international expansion opportunities.
